About this tool

A calmer, more accessible way to find nearby services.

This map helps people — especially seniors — find services and places across Vancouver. It uses open data from the City of Vancouver Open Data Portal and is maintained for the West End Seniors' Network.

The interface prioritizes readable type, clear controls, and quick directions so members can browse with less friction on desktop, tablet, or phone.

Tiles by OpenStreetMap contributors. Map library: Leaflet.
